If you post on Bluesky regularly, you've probably wanted to write a few posts in one sitting and
have them go out over the week. Here's how to do that with Postfalcon, step by step. Scheduling
to Bluesky is free on postfalcon.co, with no card needed.

## What you need first: an app password

Bluesky doesn't let other apps sign in with your real password, and it doesn't use the "Sign in
with…" button flow most platforms have. Instead you create an **app password**: a separate
password that only works for apps, which you can delete at any time without touching your main
login.

1. In Bluesky, open **Settings → Privacy and security → App passwords** (or go straight to
   [bsky.app/settings/app-passwords](https://bsky.app/settings/app-passwords)).
2. Click **Add app password** and give it a name you'll recognise, like "Postfalcon".
3. Copy the password it shows you. It looks like `xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx`, and Bluesky only shows it
   once.

## Connect Bluesky to Postfalcon

1. [Sign up](/signup) (or log in) and open **Accounts**.
2. In the **Bluesky** card, enter your handle (for example `you.bsky.social`) and the app
   password you just made.
3. Click **Connect Bluesky**. Your account appears in the list straight away.

If you ever want to cut Postfalcon off, delete the app password in Bluesky's settings. Nothing
else changes.

## Schedule your first post

1. On the **Dashboard**, click a day on the calendar (or **New post**).
2. Pick your Bluesky account and write the post. The counter shows how much of Bluesky's
   **300-character** limit you've used.
3. Add up to **4 images** if you like, each with alt text, which Bluesky shows to people using
   screen readers.
4. Choose the date and time, then **Schedule**.

Postfalcon publishes it at that time, whether or not your computer is on. Links, @mentions and
#hashtags come out clickable, just as if you'd typed them in the Bluesky app.

A few things to know:

- **Images, not video (yet).** Video posts to Bluesky aren't supported yet; attach images or
  post video from the app.
- **Times are in your own time zone.** The calendar shows them the way you'd expect, and
  Postfalcon handles the conversion.
- **Something went wrong?** If a post can't be published (say you deleted the app password), it
  shows as failed on the calendar with the reason, and you can fix it and retry.

## Post to Mastodon and Telegram at the same time

Lots of people post the same thing on Bluesky and Mastodon. Connect both, tick both accounts in
the composer, and one post goes to each, with its own character limit checked separately. The
same works for a Telegram channel. See [cross-posting](/blog/cross-posting-to-multiple-platforms)
for the details.

## Posts that repeat

For something that goes out every week, like a Friday roundup or a monthly reminder, set the post to
repeat daily, weekly or monthly instead of writing it again each time. See
[recurring posts](/blog/recurring-social-media-posts).

## What it costs

Bluesky, Mastodon and Telegram are free on postfalcon.co, for up to 5 accounts across the
three, including cross-posting and repeating posts. The paid plans add the platforms that charge
or review apps (Threads, LinkedIn, Pinterest, YouTube, TikTok and more). Postfalcon is also
open source, so you can [run it on your own server](https://github.com/postfalcon/postfalcon) instead.
